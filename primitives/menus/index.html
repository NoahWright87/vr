Menus — panel ahead: point and pull the trigger. Watches on your wrists: point with one hand (squeeze grip) and poke the other wrist's face to project a menu outward from it. Tilt that wrist toward your own face to poke its options directly; turn the face up instead and it grows bigger and turns to face you, so your other hand's laser can select from it. Poke the ❌ (or just lower the wrist) to close it. All prototypes
