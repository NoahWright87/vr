Menus — panel ahead: point and pull the trigger (or any face button). Watches on your wrists: point with one hand (squeeze grip) and poke the other wrist's face to project a menu outward from it. Tilt that wrist toward your own face to poke its options directly; turn the face up instead and it grows bigger and turns to face you. Either way, pointing and pulling the trigger/a face button always works too — poke the ❌ or point-and-activate it (or just lower the wrist) to close it. To your right, a wall screen; to your left, a pedestal button — poke either to project a menu outward, then poke or point-and-activate its options. Each ❔ shows a different help style: the watch's swaps in a paged modal, the wall's opens a sidecar panel beside the screen, the pedestal's draws a tutorial overlay on top (step it with any A/X or B/Y face button). Walking far enough away, or looking away for a few seconds, auto-closes a menu. All prototypes
