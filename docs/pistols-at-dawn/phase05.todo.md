# Phase 05 and beyond (sketch)

A parking lot, not a plan. Everything here is agreed to be part of the
vision, roughly in order. Since this is a living project, the list is
expected to keep growing. Rewrite as earlier phases land.

- **Roll the Duel.** Weapons × times × places × modifiers as real
  cards, a title generator, a slot-machine pick for PvP, the host's
  deck for the room (`deck.todo.md`).
- **The wagon and places off the strip.** Rides to the desert, canyon,
  train and moon, following the comfort rules. Places built from
  modifiers first, new maps only when a modifier can't do it
  (`town.todo.md`).
- **Shootout locations.** Turn the Bank and other shell buildings into
  arenas (`town.todo.md`).
- **More saloon activities.** Pool, a playable piano, drinking games,
  bar fights, five finger fillet, poker (`activities.todo.md`).
- **More shooting activities.** Trick-shot sets, timed target courses,
  trick-shot H-O-R-S-E.
- **Horses.** Prototype the riding comfort first, then care and
  training, racing, and riding around with friends into NPC shootouts
  (`animals.todo.md`).
- **The farm and the lasso.** Animals to tend, lassoing as a shared
  verb first and an activity second.
- **Zany weapons.** Blunderbuss, muskets, hand-loaded cannons, ninja
  stars (TODO.md "Weapons not built yet").
- **Moving platforms** (parade floats, rush-hour traffic), built to
  the comfort rules in `deck.todo.md`.
- **Syncing fire, liquids and explosions over the network**, by
  sending only starting events.
- **Gang duels, then co-op shootouts** against several AI opponents.
  Needs cover and movement logic the duel AI never needed.
- **Non-VR players in multiplayer**, once the shared semantic-hands
  work in `common/` covers Pistols' verbs (`platforms.todo.md`).
- **Slide-into-cover move**, if style and co-op want it.
- **Money sinks.** Golden-toilet-tier items and town upgrades.
- **Swapping in real assets.** Work through the placeholder manifest
  (`aesthetic.todo.md`): models, textures, sound effects, music, the
  recorded voice sound set and catchphrases (`cast.todo.md`).
