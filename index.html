WebXR Prototypes

Small self-contained WebXR experiments, built for the Meta Quest Browser. Each one lives in its own folder as a single HTML file with no build step.

  • Cube Pop Point a controller at a floating cube and pull the trigger to pop it. Pop them all to win.
  • Pistols at Dawn Draw a holstered pistol, shotgun, or your hat and shoot up a whole gallery — stationary targets, spinning wheels, conveyor belts, and pop-up surprises. Twirl anything you're holding, toss it hand to hand, or snap it into any anchor it fits — including a gun tucked inside your hat, or worn on your head like one. Turn around for the saloon: knock a cap off on the counter, pour the beer into your own face, throw a fistful of bottles up and shoot them to pieces, light a cigar off your own hot barrel, and find out what happens to your aim when you overdo either. Spilled beer pools and burns, fire spreads along it and takes the targets with it, and a thrown bottle spills its own — so the Molotov builds itself out of parts that were already there.
