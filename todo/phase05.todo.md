# Phase 05 and beyond (sketch)

A parking lot, not a plan. Things everyone agreed are part of the
vision, in rough order. Rewrite as earlier phases land.

- **The deck and Roll the Duel.** Weapons × times × places × modifiers
  as real cards, title generator, slot-machine pick for PvP, the
  host's deck for the room (`deck.todo.md`).
- **Zany places, cheaply.** Places built from modifiers first (the
  moon = desert + low gravity + black sky), new maps only when a
  modifier can't do it.
- **Zany weapons.** Blunderbuss, muskets, hand-loaded cannons, ninja
  stars (see TODO.md "Weapons not built yet").
- **Moving platforms** (parade floats, rush-hour traffic), built to
  the comfort rules in `deck.todo.md`.
- **Syncing fire, liquids and explosions over the network**, by
  sending only starting events.
- **Gang duels, then co-op shootouts** against several AI opponents.
  Needs cover and movement logic the duel AI never needed.
- **Slide-into-cover move**, if style and co-op want it.
- **Money sinks.** Golden-toilet-tier items and town upgrades: paying
  to fix up the attraction's empty buildings.
- **Owned gun finishes** (gold plating), once the ownership question in
  `economy.todo.md` is answered.
- **Polish.** Models, textures, sound effects and music from asset
  bundles. Recording the voice sound set and catchphrases, using
  speech-to-speech voice changing (`cast.todo.md`).
