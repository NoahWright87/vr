Magic Hands

Put the controllers down. Your hands are the magic.

Learn the three spells
  • Lightning bolt — point your index finger, curl the other three fingers, lift your thumb. Wait for the blue glow, then lower your thumb toward the index knuckle. Lift it again to reload.
  • Farsight — curl your spare fingers, touch index fingertips at the top and thumb tips at the bottom to make a round window. Hold it in front of your eyes and look through it. A smaller opening gives more zoom, up to 16×.
  • Shield — extend all four fingers and turn your palm upward or forward, in front of you. Each hand makes a 0.5 m shield; bring both open hands within 40 cm for a 1 m shield.

Enable hand tracking on your Quest, then enter VR. A brief hold settles each spell. Tracking loss releases it; lightning needs a fresh thumb lift.

Desktop rehearsal: drag to look, WASD to move. Click the scene or press Space to drop the thumb. Release to reload. Scroll changes the circle size. H switches one/two shield hands.
