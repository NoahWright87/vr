Magic Hands

Put the controllers down. Your hands are the magic.

Ready to spar? Portal to the arena off to your right. Enemies stay inside. Block headbutts and slow orbs with your shield; a broken shield needs a second and a fresh palm-out pose. Damage flashes red, with no player HP. Raise fallen enemies with 🤘 + lift.

Learn the eight spells
  • Lightning bolt — point your index finger and loosely curl the others. The blue guide averages the direction of your whole hand and index finger; a bright marker shows aim assistance. Fold your thumb roughly parallel to your finger and hold it there for weaker automatic bolts. Raise it to stop. Either hand works independently.
  • Farsight — touch fingertips above and the heels of your hands below to make a circle or diamond. Thumb contact and exact finger shapes do not matter. Bring the window close to your face for about 10× zoom, or extend your arms for about 2×.
  • Shield — extend an open hand to roughly 80% of your arm's reach with the palm facing outward. Each hand makes a 0.5 m shield; bring both extended open hands within 40 cm for a 1 m shield. Retract to dismiss.
  • Boulder fists — close an empty fist and wait for stone to grow around it. Punch a nearby target or loose stone, then retract to punch again. Relax your hand to shed the boulder.
  • Fireball — briefly hold a cupped palm upward, or scoop a little, to gather fire. Throw or simply thrust forward; exact finger opening is unnecessary. Look toward the target to guide the throw. You can gather another immediately. Open palm-down at rest to dismiss it.
  • Telekinesis — point at a highlighted rune prop and pinch to lift. Yank your hand back, then open it like a glove to summon and catch the prop. Push forward to throw. Close then open to release, or turn an open catching hand palm-down. Gentle releases hover; thrown props settle and float back up. Close your hand or pinch around a nearby prop to grab it directly, even while wearing a boulder.
  • Portal — bring your open hands close together at chest height, backs together and palms facing out. Look toward a destination; hand direction adds a small aiming bias. Simply spread your hands apart to open a rectangular doorway. Grab either side with a fist or pinch to keep it open, then pull back to travel, or step through. The exit faces your old location. Unheld portals shrink away. The back is solid; nested entrances show static.
  • Necromancy — visit the small graveyard behind the starting point. Extend index + pinky and curl the middle/ring fingers (🤘); your thumb does not matter. Eligible graves glow. Look toward a grave and lift your upright hand to raise a skeleton. Up to three minions follow and wander nearby. Raising a fourth makes the oldest collapse and sink. Point the horns toward a marked target to order an attack; point down to call the group back.

Enable hand tracking on your Quest, then enter VR. Held spells survive brief hand occlusion for 1.5 seconds. Unseen hands cannot start new actions; lightning pauses until tracking returns.

Desktop: drag to look, WASD to move. Space/click uses the right hand; Q uses the left. Lightning: hold to fire automatically. Boulders: hold to punch, release to retract. Fire: hold to scoop, release to throw; repeat without resetting. Telekinesis: hold to pinch, G yanks then opens a catching glove, T pushes to throw. Farsight: scroll or slide face distance. Shield: slide arm extension; H switches one/two hands.

Portal: 7 brings both palms out. Look toward clear ground, then hold Space/click to spread the hands apart. Release Space, then hold G to grab a side and pull through.

Necromancy: 8 makes horns. Turn toward the graveyard behind you; hold Space to lift and raise, then release to lower. Turn toward the targets and press G to point/attack, or T to point down/recall. Q lifts the left hand.
