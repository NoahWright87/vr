Pistols at Dawn — grip near a holstered pistol, shotgun, or your hat to draw it. Guns fire on the trigger; let go of the grip while a finger still rests on the trigger to dangle and twirl whatever you're holding. Anything can snap into any anchor it's small enough for — glance at the glowing spheres for a hint — so go ahead and wear a gun like a hat, or hide one inside your hat. Turn around for the saloon bar: knock a bottle's cap on the counter edge to open it, then tip it over your face and drink what pours out. Fling a fistful of bottles skyward and shoot them apart. Strike a match on the counter or flip open the lighter and thumb a face button, then light a cigar off either — or off a barrel you just emptied, or a well-aimed bullet. Hold a cigar at your lips to draw on it and again to blow the smoke back out; flick or twirl it to knock the ash off. Let go and re-squeeze the grip quickly to keep hold of what you had and pick up the next thing too. Grab the shotgun's forend with your off hand to steady it. Spilled beer pools where it lands and burns if you light it, fire spreads along it and sets light to whatever it reaches, and a smashed bottle spills its own — so a lit puddle and a well-thrown beer make a Molotov out of parts that were already lying about. There's a jug of water for when that goes wrong — and yes, since fire is a liquid and liquid that reaches your mouth gets swallowed, you can drink it. Throw overhand and it goes where you're looking; let go early to lob it, late to line-drive it. Both meters at the bottom of your view have opinions about your aim. Shoot the brass bell hanging over the bar and the whole counter revolves — beers, cigars, lit matches and all — to show an armoury on its back: guns on the pegs, long guns in the cradles, and a lot of empty sockets to arrange your own loadout in. The armoury's racks refill ten seconds after you empty them; your own holsters don't, so what's on your hip is whatever you put there. Any gun left lying loose goes up in smoke half a minute later — take what you want, and don't put it down. All prototypes
