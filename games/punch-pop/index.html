Punch Pop — punching is movement. A swing only lunges you if it's roughly aimed where you're looking (prevents accidental backward punches); if an enemy is close by and near your aim it locks on, otherwise you move halfway between your punch and look directions. Punch a cube hard enough to pop it — any direction, including up. Press either grip to open the menu, a couple feet in front of you — your other hand gets a laser pointer to click with; press either grip again to close it. Outside the menu, pull either trigger for a quick reset. Clear ~2x2m of real space before trying this in a headset. All prototypes
