Punch Pop — punching is movement. Swing to lunge, uppercut to launch, hammer down to crash. Punch a cube hard enough to pop it. Hold either grip to check the watch menu on that wrist — it disables punching and gives your other hand a laser pointer to click with (speed, cube count, behavior, Exit VR); release the grip to close it. Outside the menu, pull either trigger for a quick reset. Clear ~2x2m of real space before trying this in a headset. All prototypes
